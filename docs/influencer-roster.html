A recurring format in Italian skincare content

Routine captions recur among Italy-based skincare creators. On 26 Nov we checked the three featured Instagram posts per Italy-labeled profile in Modash’s 11 Nov 2026 skincare list: 5 of 57 captions contained “routine” (case-insensitive, hashtags included; 19 profiles, one Swiss profile excluded). Matches: Elisa’s hair routine, @skincare_opinion, and three from @basya_skincare (1, 2, 3). Method: use the public list’s popularPosts captions, not all posts or its separate Reels field. This is a recurring format in a selected sample, not a growth rate or evidence of toothbrush demand.

There is also a reason to test a short demo: in its 27 Apr 2026 first-year report, TikTok says shoppable video accounted for 38% of TikTok Shop Italy turnover since launch, versus 20% for LIVE. That is format context, not an Instagram sales forecast.
