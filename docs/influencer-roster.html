Influencer Shortlist: Oral-B iO · Evidence and sources

Where each number comes from

Followers: Instagram followers shown on the linked Modash entry for that handle. We keep large and small creators for different roles. A 300,000-follower threshold was a scouting preference, not a condition for joining the roster.

Engagement rate: Modash’s methodology (21 Oct 2026) defines Instagram ER as median likes divided by followers, expressed as a percentage, using roughly the last three months of content. Its TikTok rate uses views as the denominator. The two rates are not interchangeable.

Plays per follower: average Instagram Reel plays ÷ Instagram followers, using the displayed counts. For Dadda, 955.4k ÷ 752.4k = 1.27. For Vincenzo, 368.9k ÷ 374.3k = 0.99. A person can play a Reel more than once: this ratio does not measure unique people reached.

Famiglia Calò: the 25 Nov Instagram entry gives 1.25% ER. Their TikTok account is @famigliacalo. We do not use a TikTok follower count as the denominator for an Instagram rate.

Why try a routine video?

On 26 Nov we checked three featured Instagram captions for each Italy-labeled profile in the Modash skincare list. Five of 57 captions contained “routine,” including hashtags. One was Elisa’s “Hair routine”; the other four used the word in hashtags. The dated tally and post links show all 20 profiles; one Swiss profile was excluded. This supports testing a familiar format, not a claim that toothbrush demand is rising.

There is a commercial reason to try short video too: TikTok Shop Italy’s first-year report (27 Apr 2026) attributes 38% of its turnover since launch to shoppable videos and 20% to LIVE. These are shares of TikTok Shop turnover, not predicted Oral-B sales.

Before booking, ask each creator to confirm current audience location and native account insights. Do not add Instagram and TikTok audiences or treat unverified estimates of fake followers as facts about a creator.

Influencer Shortlist: Oral-B iO · Activation and measurement

The trend we create:
#iOSmileUnlocked / #SorrisoSbloccato

These are our proposed campaign hashtags. Coco Genge launches the trend on TikTok and Instagram. Each creator films one video and posts it on both platforms. Fitness creators adapt it after a workout; family creators make a parent-led version with iO Kids.

How it works

  1. Hook: the creator films a messy morning face with the text “not unlocked yet.”
  2. Brushing: fast-cut a two-minute routine to music. Show the green pressure cue only on a model that supports it (Oral-B’s iO model guide).
  3. Reveal: where the featured model displays a smiley, frame it beside the creator’s smile. Otherwise use the creator’s smile and caption: “Smile unlocked” / “Sorriso sbloccato.”

Creative freedom: give creators the idea, not a full script. A recognisable reveal links the posts while leaving each creator room for their own style.

Campaign KPIs · proposed 14-day readout

Measure the first 14 days after each post goes live. Give each creator a separate tagged link for Instagram and TikTok. Keep platform results separate. The window and measures below are our proposal, not published benchmarks.

QuestionMeasure and source
Did people visit?Count iO product-page sessions arriving through each creator/platform link. Use session campaign and source, with a distinct creator ID in utm_content. Google Analytics: campaign URLs.
Did visitors order?Completed iO orders in those tagged landing sessions ÷ tagged landing sessions. Count distinct order IDs containing an iO SKU in the same session and window. Publish orders, sessions and the ratio. Google Analytics: purchase event, items and transaction ID.

With zero sessions the ratio is unavailable. If the retailer cannot supply completed order data, report visits only. These are attributed orders, not incremental sales or proof of purchase by viewers.
