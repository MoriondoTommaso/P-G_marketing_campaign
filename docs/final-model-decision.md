## 1. Why the forest, and what would change our confidence?

The business decision is whether to spend €1,500 for a chance at €2,500 incremental sales. Ranking matters for prioritization; probability accuracy and **observed held-out net at the strict 60% threshold** matter for funding. A higher ROC-AUC alone would not justify this choice.

| Historical test (same 170 labeled stores) | Forest net | Tree net | Logistic net | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Five protocol store-ID folds; current features/learners | €90,500 | €86,000 | €84,500 | Forest Brier/AUC **0.0646/0.9413**; tree **0.0918/0.8533**. |
| 11 stratified store-ID assignments, mean | €89,455 | €87,045 | €83,864 | Forest beats tree on net 9 times, ties once, loses once; better Brier and AUC in all 11. |
| Hold out one geographic cell; exclude training stores within 15 km | €82,000 | €83,500 | €83,500 | Forest ranks better but earns less at the funding boundary. Median separation is close to that of new sites. |
| Harder 30 km multi-cell stress, 11 assignments, mean | €77,909 | €85,909 | €78,955 | Tree wins on net in 10/11. New-site profitability is **not** established. |

The observed activate-all/none controls are €15,000/€0. These repeated tests reuse the same selectively activated stores; they are not independent prospective trials. The family and feature amendments were made after seeing historical outcomes. Keep that disclosure in the methods and deck.

**Feature story to present:** Dentist distance is the dominant historical signal. Shuffling it in held-out folds cost the fitted forest €60,150 in mean case net; shuffling any single other feature cost at most €4,550. Yet retraining the forest with all five rather than distance alone improved net by €12,500 on the ID split and €8,500 on the 15 km geographic test. Among same-fold success/failure pairs tied by the tree on the ID split, the forest correctly ordered 155 of 211 (11 ties). In all five standardized logistic fits, dentist distance had the largest absolute coefficient and school count the second; greater distance was associated with higher failure odds. Use these as evidence of **predictive associations**, not causal drivers, additive monetary contributions or a known distance unit.

Sources: [independent model comparison](model-review/model-selection-2026-11-26.md), [held-out feature importance, retrained models and logistic coefficients](model-review/forest-interpretability.md), [metrics](../validation/T10/model-selection-2026-11-26/metrics.csv) and [store-level held-out evidence](../validation/T10/model-selection-2026-11-26/held-out-scores.csv).

## 3. Publish one financial package or publish nothing

1. From the validated national scores compute `expected_net_eur = 2500 × p_success − 1500`. For selected IDs: expected sales = `2500 × sum(p_success)`, spend = `1500 × count`, net = sales − spend. Historical backtest net uses **observed labels**, not these forecasts. The actual 2026 campaign remains 170 stores, €255,000 spend and €15,000 net.
2. Rebuild `outputs/national-scores-class.csv` and `activation-list-class.csv` with their existing five-column interface. Generate or independently regenerate and verify `outputs/financial-summary-class.csv` **from this run**. Report optimized forecast versus actual 2026 and versus forecast repeat-170; for equal spend use `k=min(selected_count,170)` and compare the top `k` qualifying national scores against the top `k` historical scores, with the protocol's ID tie-break. Include cohort subtotals, zero-selection handling and sensitivity. Never compare models by which one forecasts more profit for its own selections.
3. Put a **shared, blocking validation step before every published writer**, including the seven existing CSV writers and the finance summary. Stage computed artifacts outside the submission directory and publish the complete package only after all validations and writes succeed. Do not use an old summary or an existing output file as input. Persist keyed folds, OOF scores/votes, upstream refit scores/votes, metrics and sensitivity as release QA evidence in a versioned location distinct from RC3 exports.
4. **Fault tests, one per clean empty destination:** unmatched or conflicting activation, duplicate store, missing/duplicate national score, inverted membership, altered upstream-to-release vote/score, incorrect historical spend, contradictory finance table, or failure in a late branch. Every test must fail and leave **zero published files**. A clean run must publish the entire declared manifest. Check exact ID sets, values, cohorts and policy arithmetic; counts and hashes alone do not catch a consistently wrong scorer.
5. Team 4 independently imports the candidate, resets and executes all in KNIME 5.12.1, recalculates finance from the fresh store rows, then records input, workflow, evidence and output SHA-256s, commit, software version, warnings and reviewer. Compare `outputs/rerun/` against the **new** reference package. Repeat T13/T15 for this candidate; prior tree sign-off applies only to its original hashes.
