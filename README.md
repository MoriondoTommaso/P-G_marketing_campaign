# P&G Oral-B Italy 2027 — Marketing Campaign

**LUISS Guido Carli | Business and Marketing Analytics**

## Project Overview

This repository contains a group project developed for the Business and Marketing Analytics course at LUISS Guido Carli, based on a marketing challenge proposed by Procter & Gamble (P&G).

The objective is to develop a data-driven strategy for Oral-B's 2027 marketing campaign in Italy, combining predictive analytics, financial evaluation, and consumer marketing insights.

## Objectives

- Analyze historical campaign performance and retail store characteristics.
- Identify the stores with the highest expected value for future marketing activations.
- Develop and evaluate predictive models using KNIME Analytics Platform.
- Estimate campaign costs, expected incremental sales, and financial outcomes.
- Propose actionable marketing strategies to improve consumer engagement.

## Project Organization

The project is divided into four workstreams:

| Team | Area | Responsibilities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Research & Methodology | Project setup, statistical evaluation, and consumer research |
| 2 | Data Analytics | Data preparation, exploratory analysis, and financial baseline |
| 3 | Predictive Modeling | Machine learning, model evaluation, and store targeting |
| 4 | Marketing & Delivery | Marketing recommendations, quality assurance, and final presentation |

Three additional activities are included: external data research, influencer analysis, and a generative AI consumer experience concept.

## Tools & Methodology

The project uses KNIME Analytics Platform, Excel datasets, statistical analysis, machine learning, and business-oriented financial evaluation.
